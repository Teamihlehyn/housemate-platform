## 1. What Peabudy is, and isn't

- Peabudy helps people looking to share a home in **London** and **Lagos** find compatible people to live with, talk to them, and write down what they agree about rent, bills and notice.
- **Peabudy isn't a landlord, letting agent, estate agent or property manager.** We don't list homes, arrange viewings, collect rent or deposits, or create tenancies.
- **Your household agreement is a record of what you and the other person said. It isn't a tenancy or a legally binding contract,** and it doesn't replace a tenancy agreement with a landlord.
- This is **early access**. Features may change, pause or stop, and we open areas one at a time.

## 2. Who can use it

You must:
- be **18 or over**
- be looking for a shared home for yourself in a city we serve
- use your real name and your own email address
- have one account only.

Peabudy is **for individuals, not agents, landlords or businesses** advertising property during early access.

## 3. Identity check

Before you can see other members or be seen, someone from our team checks your photo ID on a short video call. We record only whether it passed and when. We don't keep a copy (see the privacy notice).

**A completed check means we saw an ID that matched the person on the call. It doesn't mean that person is safe, honest or a good housemate.** Checks completed — not a guarantee of safety.

We may decline or reverse a check, for example if we think the details were false.

Use your own judgement. Have a video call before meeting, meet in public first, and **never pay anything before you've seen a home in person and checked who the landlord or agent is.**
