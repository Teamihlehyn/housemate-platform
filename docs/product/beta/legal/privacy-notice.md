## 2. The short version

- We use your data to find you compatible people to live with, let you talk to them, and help you agree how you'll share a home.
- Before anyone can see other members or be seen, someone from our team checks your photo ID on a short video call. **We don't keep a copy of your ID.** We record only that the check passed or failed, and when.
- Other members see your public profile only. They never see your email, phone number, gender, who you're open to living with, or any preferred address.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't show adverts.
- Peabudy is for people aged **18 or over**.

## 8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, and under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data (in Settings, "Export my data", or by email)
- correct it (most of it you can edit yourself in Profile)
- delete it (Settings, "Delete account"; your profile is hidden immediately)
- restrict or object to how we use it, including anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests
- move it to another service (the export is a machine-readable file)
- withdraw consent at any time (gender preference, surveys, waitlist, usage recordings). Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before.

**How:** email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We may ask you to confirm it's you. We'll reply within **one month** [NIGERIA: confirm the NDPA response period], and we'll tell you if we need longer.

**Complaints:** please tell us first. You can also complain to:
- the **Information Commissioner's Office** (UK), ico.org.uk
- the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**, ndpc.gov.ng

## 9. Age

Peabudy is only for people **18 or over**. You confirm this when you sign up, and we check it during the ID call. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we close it and delete their data, except any safety record we need to keep.

## 10. Security

We use encrypted connections. Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it. Every change to an account is logged. Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email. No system is perfectly secure: if we have a data breach that puts you at risk, we'll tell you and the regulators as the law requires.
