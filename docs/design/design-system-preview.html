Calm ledger

The Peabudy design system: the calm, caring warmth of a wellness app with the clear, confident money display of a good banking app. Warm paper, ink type, sage green for agreement, and one big number per money card.

Principles

Show what's true

Every check, sample and demo carries its fixed honesty phrase, at full size.

One screen, one job

One primary action. Quiet secondary actions. Destructive actions set apart.

Money at a glance

The amount is the biggest thing on the card, in tabular figures, with its period.

Calm by default

Warm space, soft corners. No countdowns, streaks or "3 people viewing".

Mutual, not one-sided

Every shared decision shows both people's status side by side.

Everyone can use it

WCAG 2.2 AA, 44 px targets, 16 px body, fast on a low-end phone.

Colour

Ratios use the WCAG 2.x formula. Text needs 4.5:1; controls and focus rings need 3:1. Colour never carries meaning alone.

Peabudy logo and mascots

Chosen direction (Nov 2026): the Pod. Two peas in one pod: two housemates, one home. The mark is a sage tile with an open pod and two peas. The wordmark is a custom monoline drawn from circles and stems, so it needs no font file. Exports: public/peabudy-logo.svg, peabudy-logo-light.svg, peabudy-mark.svg, peabudy-wordmark.svg and app/icon.svg (faces removed at favicon size). Run a trademark search before final adoption.

Pod mark In use

Works in colour at 16 px; the faces drop out below 24 px.

Pea and Bud

The two mascots. Pea asks the onboarding questions. On the landing page, tapping either one makes it say something true about how Peabudy works.

Moods

sleep, awake, happy, wow, wink. Sleeping peas wake as living-habit questions are answered. Never used to signal safety or compatibility.

Earlier concepts (superseded by the Pod)

1 · Two doors Recommended

Two arched doorways under one roof: two people with their own space, one shared home.

2 · Gable H

An H whose stems meet in a roof. The initial and the house in one stroke, and the clearest at 16 px.

3 · Common ground

Two circles overlapping, with the shared part filled. It shows how we match: on what overlaps, never a score.

4 · Signed roof

A roofline that runs on into a signature, with a line beneath it: agree in writing before you move in.

5 · Lights on

A four-pane window with two panes lit: two people at home, each in their own room.

6 · Talk first

A house shaped like a speech bubble, with two people in it: talk it through before you share a home.

Do and don't

Do

  • "Checks completed — not a guarantee of safety" next to every score
  • £950/mo with tabular figures and a period
  • "Not for me", quiet and undoable
  • Both people's acceptance shown side by side

Don't

  • "92% compatible" or "Verified & safe"
  • Swipe stacks, streaks, countdowns
  • Red for decline, or asking why someone declined
  • backdrop-blur and heavy blurred shadows
